About Us

GastroApp

GastroApp is a recipe management platform designed for efficiency and control. We provide a clean, searchable database of recipes that users can browse, and administrators can fully manage.

What we offer

  • Complete recipe catalog with images, ingredients and step-by-step instructions
  • Search and filter functionality to find recipes quickly
  • Administrator panel with full edit and delete capabilities
  • Clean interface without distractions or unnecessary elements

For administrators

The platform includes a protected admin area where authorized users can edit recipe names, delete outdated entries, and maintain the database directly through the interface. All changes are applied immediately.

A straightforward tool for managing and discovering recipes. No complexity, just what you need to cook and keep things organized.
